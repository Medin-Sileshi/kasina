# Content coverage status

Honest inventory of what Kasina actually ships in the question bank and textbooks today versus what marketing or grant language may imply.

## Live today

| Grade | Subject | Question bank | Textbook reader |
|-------|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| 12 | Mathematics | Yes (`@kasina/question-bank`) | Yes (R2 / `content/textbooks`) |

This is a deliberate, tightly scoped starting point — not an accidental gap.

## DAP pilot target (Grade 11–12)

| Need | Status |
|------|--------|
| Grade 12 Mathematics | **Live** |
| Grade 11 Mathematics | Planned before / during pilot expansion — tracked workstream, not yet in bank |
| Other national-exam subjects (e.g. Physics, Chemistry, English) | **Post-pilot** unless explicitly funded and scheduled |

If the pilot cohort must sit exams beyond Grade 12 Math, expanding `packages/question-bank` is a **dated content workstream**, not something to discover after schools are onboarded.

## Post-pilot

- Grades 9–10 expansion (product vision)
- Additional subjects and Amharic-first item coverage growth
- Offline Android Melak app (`apps/mobile`)

## How to talk about this externally

Prefer: “Currently focused on Grade 12 Mathematics, with Grade 11–12 pilot expansion planned.”

Avoid implying full Grade 9–12 multi-subject coverage is already in product.
