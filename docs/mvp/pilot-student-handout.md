# Kasina — Student handout (3 steps)

**You need internet.** Airplane mode will not work.

**Invite code from your teacher:** ________________

---

## 1. Open the site

Go to **https://kasina.et**  
(or the link your teacher shared).

Tap **Get Started** or **Join class**.

---

## 2. Join with the invite code

Enter:

1. **Invite code** (from the board / chat)  
2. **Your full name**  
3. **Email** + **password** (remember these)

Tap **Continue** / **Join class**.

Already joined before? Use **Student login** on the home page instead.

---

## 3. Do your assignment

On **Home**, find **Assigned for you**.

Tap **Start** → answer each question → finish the quiz.

You can also **Practice Mathematics** on your own and check **My Progress**.

---

**Stuck?** Message the class support channel your teacher named (Telegram / WhatsApp / email).
