# Data handling & child protection

Kasina’s pilot serves Ethiopian secondary students, many of whom are **minors**. This is an operational policy statement for product and grant compliance — not a substitute for formal legal advice.

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Name, phone number | Account identity (phone OTP) |
| School affiliation | Pilot eligibility (government schools) and reporting |
| Role (student / teacher / admin) | Access control |
| Class membership | Classroom features |
| Quiz / CBT answers, scores, timing | Learning support and teacher insight |
| Melak chat messages (when persisted) | Tutor continuity and quality |
| Device/sync metadata | Offline sync and support |
| Admin audit actions | Accountability for approve/reject/revoke |

We do **not** require email for student login. We do not sell student data.

## Who can access it

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Student | Own progress, sessions, Melak history |
| Teacher | Own classes’ students, assignments, results |
| Admin | Signup approval, school-level overview, revoke, audit trail |
| Operators (Kasina team) | Infrastructure logs and support — minimum needed |

## Retention

| Category | Retention |
|----------|-----------|
| Active pilot accounts & learning data | Duration of pilot + up to **24 months** for M&E unless deletion is requested earlier |
| Rejected signup requests | Up to **90 days**, then delete or anonymize (enough to handle appeals / abuse review) |
| Revoked accounts | Credentials disabled immediately; learning records retained per pilot M&E needs unless deletion requested |
| Server logs | Short operational window (typically ≤ 30 days) |

Exact cutoffs may be tightened when partner schools or funders require it.

## Consent & safeguarding

- School / teacher onboarding should inform students (and where required, guardians) that learning activity is stored to support teaching and pilot evaluation.
- Melak and practice tools are educational aids — not counselling or emergency services.
- Report safeguarding concerns through the school’s existing child-protection channels; Kasina admins escalate to school contacts when misuse or welfare risk appears in-product.

## Parent / guardian deletion requests

A parent, guardian, or school administrator may request deletion of a student’s personal data by contacting the Kasina pilot operator (email on [kasina.et/contact](https://kasina.et/contact) / support channel).

We will:

1. Verify the request with the partner school where possible.
2. Disable the account.
3. Delete or anonymize personal identifiers and Melak history within a reasonable period (target **30 days**), except where law or an active investigation requires holding records.
